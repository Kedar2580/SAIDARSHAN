Frequently Asked Questions

Home  /  FAQ
Are PVC panels waterproof? +
Yes. All our PVC wall panels and soffit products are 100% waterproof, making them ideal for kitchens, bathrooms, balconies and ceilings.
Do you offer custom sizes? +
Yes, custom sizes are available on request for bulk and project orders. Share your measurements and we will guide you on the best option.
How are PVC panels installed? +
Panels can be installed using strong adhesive on flat surfaces, or fixed over a wooden / metal framework. No special skill is needed for basic fitting.
Can PVC panels be used outside? +
Yes. Our soffit panels and UV-coated products are weather-resistant and UV-stable for outdoor ceilings, fascias and under-eaves. For direct outer walls, confirm the right product with our team.
What is the minimum order quantity? +
We supply from a few panels for home use to full container / truckload quantities for dealers. Contact us for current MOQ per product.
Do you deliver outside your city? +
Yes, we deliver across India through reliable transport partners. Delivery time and charges depend on your location and order size.
Can I visit your showroom or yard to see the products? +
Certainly. Visit our location during business hours (Mon–Sat, 9AM–7PM) or call ahead so we can keep samples ready for you.
How do I become a dealer? +
Fill in the dealer enquiry form on the Become a Dealer page, and our team will contact you with pricing and partnership options.
