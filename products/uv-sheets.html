UV Sheets

Our UV sheets are finished with a special UV coating that gives a high-gloss, mirror-like surface while resisting scratches and fading. Ideal for wall cladding, false ceilings, kitchen and wardrobe shutters, and retail interiors that need a bright, contemporary look.
