WPC Louvers

WPC (Wood-Plastic Composite) louvers give you the rich look of natural timber with none of the drawbacks — no warping, no termites, no heavy maintenance. They are perfect for accent walls, ceilings, partitions and hotel or office interiors where designers want a warm, contemporary feel.

MaterialWood & PVC composite
WidthStandard louver widths (custom on request)
FinishNatural wood, matte & textured
FeaturesTermite-proof, moisture-resistant, easy to fit
UseFeature walls, ceilings, partitions, screens
InstallationClip / screw fixing on framework
