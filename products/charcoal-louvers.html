Charcoal Louvers

Charcoal louvers bring a deep, dramatic tone to interior design. Their rich dark shade creates stunning contrast when paired with light walls, and the louver profile adds elegant vertical rhythm. Ideal for TV walls, bedroom accents, lobbies and modern office spaces.

MaterialWPC / PVC composite
ColourDeep charcoal & dark tones
FinishMatte, textured
FeaturesFade-resistant, termite-proof, low maintenance
UseTV walls, accent walls, lobbies, partitions
InstallationClip / screw fixing on framework
