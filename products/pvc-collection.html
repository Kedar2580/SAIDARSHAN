Panel Specifications At a Glance

Common Technical Details for All Designs

  • MaterialPolyvinyl Chloride (PVC) with calcium-carbonate core
  • Thickness7mm, 8mm, 9mm or 10mm depending on design
  • Standard Width200mm, 250mm (10"), 300mm (12"), 400mm (16")
  • Standard Length2900mm (9.5 ft) or 3000mm (10 ft)
  • WeightApprox. 1.2 kg/m, 18–28 kg per box
  • Joint SystemTongue & Groove (clip-in) or seamless square edge
  • FinishesWood grain, marble, 3D, fluted, plain, stone, high gloss, matte
  • InstallationDry cladding on wooden / metal frame; adhesive for flat walls
  • WaterproofYes — 100% waterproof, ideal for bathrooms & kitchens
  • Fire RatingClass B1 fire-retardant, self-extinguishing compound
  • TermiteTermite-proof and moisture-resistant
  • MaintenanceWipe with a damp cloth or mild detergent
