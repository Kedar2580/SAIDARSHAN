Fluted Panels

Fluted panels feature clean vertical ribs that catch light and add wonderful depth and texture. A favourite among interior designers, they bring a premium designer look to TV walls, wardrobes, kitchens and retail spaces — combining style with the practical benefits of PVC.

MaterialPVC / WPC composite
ProfileVertical fluted / ribbed grooves
FinishMatte & textured designer finishes
FeaturesWaterproof, termite-proof, scratch-resistant
UseTV walls, wardrobes, kitchens, retail interiors
InstallationAdhesive or framework cladding
